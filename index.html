Performance engineering approach

The goal is not simply to generate load. The goal is to produce evidence that explains system behaviour and guides engineering decisions.

01 · ModelDefine business transactions, workload mix, concurrency, arrival rate and test data.
02 · BaselineEstablish response-time, throughput, error-rate and resource baselines.
03 · ExecuteRun controlled load, stress, spike, endurance and scalability scenarios.
04 · CorrelateConnect test results with application, JVM, database, network and infrastructure signals.
05 · ImproveIdentify bottlenecks, validate fixes and communicate release-readiness risks.
